## §1. Why the ordering below is not negotiable

Three constraints chain the steps together, and each one has already cost this
project something when it was violated.

1. **Alt text and rank are set at image-upload time and Etsy v3 has no
   update-image endpoint.** Repairing either after activation means re-uploading
   the gallery of a live listing — new image ids, new URLs, on something with age
   and stats. On a draft it is free. **This is why measurement precedes repair
   and repair precedes activation, and why activation is last.**
2. **Verify by measurement, not by status code** (`CLAUDE.md`; earned by GL-22a
   Q2, where Gelato returned `200` for a change it silently dropped). A `200` on
   an `alt_text` multipart field is not evidence that Etsy stored it.
3. **Do not ship a fix for an unmeasured cause, and do not ship two changes into
   one observable.** GL-71 has two live hypotheses; a reorder shipped next to a
   dispatch dedupe makes the next permutation uninterpretable. GL-48 is the
   in-house precedent for the related trap: gate the *side effect*, never the
   branch.

## §2. Step 1 — GL-69, the alt-text read-back (live, cheap, reversible)

**Goal:** answer one question with a verbatim record — does Etsy's
`uploadListingImage` accept and persist the `alt_text` multipart field as
`upload_listing_image` now sends it?

**Method**

1. Create a **throwaway draft listing** (not `4554354628`, not any listing with
   a real gallery).
2. Upload **one** image through `etsy_client.upload_listing_image(...,
   alt_text=<a distinctive sentinel string>)`. Use a sentinel that cannot occur
   naturally, so the read-back cannot be satisfied by coincidence.
3. Read the image back with `get_listing_images` and **paste the raw response
   into the findings doc verbatim** — not a summary, not "confirmed". Assert on
   the *value*, matching the GL-57 lesson that Etsy's own returned metadata can
   lag or lie (there, `rank` transiently reported `1` on two images at once).
4. Delete the throwaway draft and the temp image.

**Two outcomes, both already scoped**

| Read-back says | Verdict | Action |
|---|---|---|
| sentinel present | GL-69 closes | proceed to §3; no code change |
| empty / absent / rejected | field name or transport is wrong | fix is **entirely inside `upload_listing_image`** — nothing else depends on it — then re-run this step before proceeding |

**Do not proceed past this step in either outcome without the verbatim record on
disk.** Nothing may re-upload `4554354628`'s gallery until it exists.
