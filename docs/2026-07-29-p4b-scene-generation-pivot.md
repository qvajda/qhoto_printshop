# P4b — scene generation moves off FLUX schnell (2026-07-29)

Amends `docs/2026-07-26-gl6-attempt3-production-readiness-plan.md` §5 (phases)
and the attempt-3 findings §10 (P4a). P0–P4a stand unchanged; this doc replaces
**how P4b generates scenes** and nothing else. No pipeline code, no compositor
change, no Etsy/Gelato call.

**Decision: Nano Banana Pro (`google/nano-banana-pro`) replaces FLUX schnell for
mockup scenes, generated by hand into `assets/mockups/inflow/`.** Artwork
generation is untouched — `pipeline/generate.py` stays schnell-only.

---

## 1. What P4b1 actually returned

72 jobs, 61 delivered (11 lost to the 60 s `Prefer: wait` window), $0.18.
Re-screened with the current `scene_screen.py` at each scene's correct
`--group`:

| group | pass | printed target | what came back |
|---|---|---|---|
| primary | **0/20** | 0.6667–0.7071 | incl. three `pri_bed` at 1.05 / 1.18 / 1.32 — *landscape* panels |
| 5x7 | 2/23 | 0.7143 | both authored, uncommitted |
| 10x24 | **0/18** | 0.4167 | best 0.5215; gaps 0.20–0.45 against a 0.02 budget |

`aspect` fails 54 of 61. Two distinct problems hide under that one number.

### 1.1 10x24 is structural

Every one of the 18 failed on aspect, and the *smallest* gap was 0.20 — twenty
percentage points, not two. schnell is not missing the target; 0.4167 is outside
the distribution it samples from. No seed budget reaches it. §10.1's advice to
"buy seeds rather than rewrite a prompt when only `aspect` is failing" was
correct for a target near the mode and is wrong here.

### 1.2 Primary was not an aspect problem, and the batch design hid it

Three of 19 primaries hit the target aspect **dead on** — gaps 0.000, 0.000,
0.010, the same ~16 % rate as P0. They died elsewhere:

| candidate | aspect | gap | killed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pri_easel_v1_emerald_s319` | 0.6682 | 0.000 | `sharp` 3.68 |
| `pri_desk_v1_emerald_s341` | 0.7054 | 0.000 | `sharp` 3.52, `frontal` 0.063 |
| `pri_hallway_v1_emerald_s330` | 0.7145 | 0.010 | `no-nested` 0.0046 |

`sharp` failed 10 of 19. Three self-inflicted causes, all visible on inspection:

1. **`COMMON` orders the light across the panel.** It ends *"Natural daylight
   rakes across the whole scene"*, and it does — a hard diagonal shadow crosses
   the key on all three candidates above. §10.3 already recorded that a deeply
   shadowed key desaturates out of the key tolerance and lands in the matte's
   ramp. The shared tail manufactures the one defect the extractor cannot
   handle, on every candidate in every batch since P0.
2. **`_framed()` is built from negations** — *"no mount and no glass… No
   glazing, no glass, no reflection, no white mat, no mount board, no second
   border."* §3's own rule is that prompts must be positive-only because FLUX
   has no negative channel. `pri_hallway` came back with a white mat: the
   most-negated noun in the string.
3. **All six primary concepts were framed**, two of them (`pri_desk`,
   `pri_easel`) frames standing on furniture — the family §10.3 records as
   0/21. P4b asks for +5 *lifestyle*; the batch read that as "framed on a wall".

Each candidate also got 1 variant × 4 seeds. The method that produced
`lifestyle_framed_wall_plant` was 3 variants screened cheaply, then **16 seeds
bought on the one variant already clean on `sharp` and `frontal`**. P4b1 spent
breadth where the documented lesson was depth.

## 3. What Nano Banana Pro returned

Two hand-run attempts, screened with the same tool at `--group primary`:

| | canvas | aspect | gap | sharp | frontal | nested | outside | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| attempt 1 (prose: "A1 format") | 1200×896 | 0.7572 | 0.0662 | 1.906 | 0.007 | 0.0 | 0.0 | FAIL — `aspect` only |
| attempt 2 (+ geometry card) | 4800×3584 | **0.6967** | **0.0000** | 1.592 | 0.001 | 0.0 | 0.0 | **PASS 9/9** |

Read against §1.2 this is decisive on both counts:

- **`sharp` 1.9 and 1.6 on framed and bare-sheet scenes.** Every framed schnell
  candidate ever screened ran 2.3–4.8, and glazing killed nine of them.
- **`nested` 0.0, no mat**, with none of the six-clause negation string. And
  attempt 1's prompt carried a negation of its own — *"The bright green should
  not be reflected to any nearby surfaces"* — which produced `outside` 0.0 and a
  clean wall. Nano Banana follows negation; schnell inverts it. That single A/B
  is the whole primary blockage.

### 3.1 Prose does not set aspect; a reference image does

Attempt 1 asked for **A1**, which is 0.7063 — inside the printed range, at the
top end. It rendered 0.7572. Naming a paper standard failed exactly as
"20cm wide by 30cm tall" failed for schnell in §10.1.

`assets/mockups/geometry_cards/` now holds one card per group — an emerald
rectangle at the **midpoint of that group's printed ratio range**, on neutral
grey, with corner registration marks and **no text** (Nano Banana renders text
well enough that a "2:3" label has a real chance of being printed on the poster
in the scene; the ratio lives in the filename). Ratios verified by measuring the
written files back out, error ≤ 0.003 %:

| card | ratio | C3 will accept |
|---|---|---|
| `geometry_card_primary_0.6869.png` | 0.6869 | 0.653–0.721 |
| `geometry_card_5x7_0.7143.png` | 0.7143 | 0.700–0.729 |
| `geometry_card_10x24_0.4167.png` | 0.4167 | 0.408–0.425 |

The emerald is `KEYS['emerald']` exactly (0,177,64), so the card doubles as a
colour reference and keys stay consistent scene to scene.

### 3.2 What attempt 2 still got wrong, and what it teaches

A fern frond crosses in front of the panel's right edge: 244 px inside
`KEY_LAB_TOL` (distance 8–32) plus 78 px at 32–80, at x 2946–2994, y 1849–1858.

The screen scored it clean — the mask is 99.995 % solid inside the quad, so
`occluders` reports **0.0**. But `extract` would put the frond *inside* the print
area and the artwork would be painted over a leaf that is visibly in front of
the poster, while the 78 px between the thresholds become feathered mid-alpha —
`occluder-opacity` territory, budget 41 px, the detector that killed the P4a
clips candidate at 568 px. Expected to fail the gate; not authored to confirm.

Two rules out of it:

- **The key colour must not appear anywhere else in the scene.** Emerald and
  green foliage are a chroma collision. Keep plants clear of the panel, or key
  plant-heavy scenes in **magenta** — which is now viable in a way it was not
  in §3, where it was condemned by a measurement bug (keying against the
  *requested* colour rather than the measured one) and never actually evaluated.
  Nano Banana reproduces a requested colour far more faithfully than FLUX did.
- **A screen pass is not a gate pass, and neither is a composite review.** This
  is the third time the metrics have been clean while the eye found the defect
  (§5, §8.2, here). The full-frame review against `background.png` stays
  mandatory.

## 4. Revised P4b

**Hand-generated, owner-in-the-loop, into `assets/mockups/inflow/`.** No batch
harness. `scene_generate.py` exists because schnell needed ~60 attempts per
usable scene; a model that lands one in one or two needs a browser and
`scene_author.py extract`, which does not care where the PNG came from —
`flat_leaning_bookstack` was authored from an ordinary photograph.

Per scene: generate → drop in `inflow/<group>/` with its provenance sidecar →
`scene_screen.py … --group <g>` → `scene_author.py extract` → `mockup_qa.py
check` → full-frame + bare-scene review → commit.

Standing targets, restated against a library of **4 primary + 2 unauthored 5x7**:

| group | have | target | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| primary/portrait | 4 | 3 flat + 7 lifestyle | **+1 flat, +5 lifestyle** |
| 5x7 | 2 (uncommitted) | 10 | +8 |
| 10x24 | 0 | 10 | +10 |

Deliberately vary two things the last two scenes did not:

- **`area`.** Attempt 2 is 0.12, near the 0.10 floor — the poster is 12 % of the
  frame and will be a postage stamp in Etsy's grid. Shipped bundles run
  0.22–0.44. One wide establishing shot is good; four is not.
- **`occluders`.** Both Nano Banana scenes are 0.0 — a bare rectangle on a bare
  wall. The matte primitive exists so props can genuinely overlap the print;
  that is what makes `flat_leaning_bookstack` read as a photograph rather than a
  paste-up.

Keep sources at **~2000–2400 px** on the long side. 4800 px renders a
supersampled warp four scenes deep, twice daily, for no gallery benefit.

## 5. Open items

1. **`scripts/scene_generate.py`'s docstring** still asserts schnell is "the only
   image model this project may use". It now contradicts `CLAUDE.md`. The file
   is modified and uncommitted on `feat/gl6-p4-scene-library` — fix it in that
   working change, not separately.
2. **Sources for existing bundles are git-ignored.** `outputs/gl6_*` and
   `outputs/attempt1_*photo.png` are in `.gitignore`, so
   `flat_leaning_bookstack` is one `git clean` from being unreproducible —
   directly against the §8.4 pure-function property. Copy the four shipped
   scenes' sources into `inflow/primary/` with sidecars.
3. **The 60 s `Prefer: wait` window** cost 11 of 72 images in P4b1 and is
   flagged unverified for real-esrgan in `replicate_client`. Unblocked for now
   by generating scenes by hand, but it is still live for the upscaler on the
   artwork path.
4. **SynthID** rides on every Nano Banana output. Not an Etsy problem — the
   artwork is already AI-disclosed via `who_made: i_did` — but it is a permanent
   marker on the store's photography and should be a conscious choice.
5. **Wiring Nano Banana into `replicate_client`** is deferred, not rejected.
   `_predict(model, input_body, api_token=…)` is already model-generic, so the
   work is a model constant, an input body, reference-image encoding, per-scene
   provenance, and the polling fallback in (3). Revisit only if seed sweeps turn
   out to be needed.
